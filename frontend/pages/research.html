Clinical Research Methodology & Governance

🛡️
Research Statement: NeuroPredict AI provides preliminary research predictions and does not replace professional medical evaluation. It is developed solely for algorithmic investigation and clinical research analytics.

1. Clinical Motivation & Cognitive Markers

Early identification of cognitive decline progression.

Alzheimer's Disease progression is characterized by gradual atrophy of cortical structures coupled with progressive cognitive impairment. The Mini-Mental State Examination (MMSE) and Clinical Dementia Rating (CDR) represent standard clinical assessments. Combining these cognitive tests with volumetric MRI indicators (Normalized Whole Brain Volume, eTIV) substantially elevates classification sensitivity.

2. Data Leakage Prevention

Methodological integrity across longitudinal cohorts.

In multi-visit longitudinal studies (e.g. ADNI, OASIS), multiple records belong to the same patient. Standard random K-Fold splits introduce severe data leakage because earlier visits contaminate training and testing folds. NeuroPredict AI strictly enforces patient-level GroupShuffleSplit, ensuring that all visits for any given subject remain exclusively within either the training or holdout test split.

3. Model Architectures & Calibration

Benchmarked predictive paradigms.

We implement and evaluate three distinct algorithmic classes:
• Logistic Regression: Linear baseline with L2 regularization and balanced class weights.
• Random Forest: Non-linear bagging ensemble of decision trees resilient to non-normal distributions.
• XGBoost: Gradient boosted decision trees optimized for subtle non-linear interactions across high-dimensional biomarker profiles.

4. Explainable AI (SHAP Framework)

Mathematical interpretability for clinical audits.

Using Shapley Additive Explanations (SHAP), the system computes cooperative game theory attributions allocating exact credit to each individual feature for every patient prediction. This illuminates whether a prediction was driven by cognitive score changes, structural brain volume atrophy, or cardiovascular risk factors.
