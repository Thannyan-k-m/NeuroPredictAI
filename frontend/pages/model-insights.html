Model Limitations, Governance & Methodological Integrity

Transparency parameters and adherence to Dexathon 2026 specifications.

  • Zero Longitudinal Data Leakage: Patient visits are partitioned using GroupShuffleSplit by subject identifier to guarantee no cross-contamination between training and holdout evaluation sets.
  • Preprocessing Pipeline: Imputation (median for numerical, most frequent for categorical) and standardization are fit strictly on training splits.
  • Research Prototype Boundary: Predictions generated are algorithmic risk stratifications and are not validated for independent medical diagnosis or intervention decisions.
